        Also supported: $image, $stylesheet, $xhr, $subdocument, $media, $font, $ping, $websocket, $~type, $first-party, $to=, $denyallow=, $method=, $match-case, $important, $all, hosts-file lines and :has(). Scriptlets, procedural selectors and $redirect are not supported.

        About

        AdBlocker runs entirely inside your browser. Requests are blocked by Chrome’s built-in declarativeNetRequest engine, settings stay on this device, and nothing about your browsing is collected or sent anywhere.
